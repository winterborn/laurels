# Image prompts: Laurels iconography

Five prompts for ChatGPT image generation, one for each icon set.
Paste one prompt into a new chat. Answer its questions, then pick a style direction.
Trace the result to SVG. The app draws icons as SVG in `public/js/Laurel.js`.

## Laurel tiers

Create a set of five festival laurel icons that show the award tier at a glance. Use one wreath shape for all five, and change only the emblem inside it. The emblem grows with the tier: one star, two stars, three stars, a star in a ring, then a star in a ring with rays. Colour each laurel in its tier metal: bronze #d29a66, silver #c9d1d9, gold #e3b04b, coral #ef8a76 and ice blue #bfe6ff. Keep the icons flat, with one line weight, solid fills and a transparent background. Do not add text, numbers, trophy cups or real award statuettes. Do not invent tiers, colours or emblems.

Ask clarifying questions to establish the sizes the icons must read at and the background they sit on. Also ask about the line weight and how ornate the leaves should be. Present relevant icon style directions when available.

## Television awards

Create a set of 14 television award icons that make each award easy to recognise without its name. Draw one icon for each award below, and show its idea in one simple object. Colour each icon in the metal of its tier. Keep the icons flat, with one line weight, solid fills and a transparent background. Do not add text or numbers. Do not draw a real TV channel mark, a real test card or a real person. Do not invent awards or change their tiers.

Use these awards:

- Pilot (bronze): your first episode
- Season pass (silver): 100 episodes
- Box set (gold): finish a whole show
- Season finale (silver): finish 5 seasons
- Binge mode (silver): 6 episodes of one show in 24 hours
- Couch potato (gold): 12 episodes in one day
- Graveyard slot (silver): 10 episodes after 2am
- Long runner (gold): 500 episodes
- Network veteran (coral): 1,000 episodes
- Test card (ice blue): 5,000 episodes
- Showrunner (coral): finish 10 shows
- Syndication deal (coral): 100 episodes of one show
- Pilot season (silver): episode 1 of 10 different shows
- Prime time (silver): 50 episodes started between 20:00 and 22:00

Ask clarifying questions to establish the object for each award and the era of the TV sets. Also ask about the grid layout and the sizes the icons must read at. Present relevant icon style directions when available.

## Flourishes and frames

Create a set of filmic ornaments that give the Laurels pages structure without adding noise. Include a section divider, a frame for the "Just announced" popup and four corner pieces for a poster frame. Also include a ballot box with a hand-drawn cross, and a small open laurel to sit next to a title. Use gold #e3b04b lines on a transparent background, with no fill. Keep each piece simple enough to repeat on every page. Do not add text, numbers, logos or real award marks.

Ask clarifying questions to establish where each piece appears, the page background, the line weight, and how much decoration the pages can carry. Present relevant ornament style directions when available, for example Art Deco, festival programme or film leader.

## Check each result

1. Count the icons. The count must match the prompt.
2. Look for text or numbers. If there are any, generate the image again.
3. Compare the line weight across the icons. It must look the same.
4. Compare each colour with its hex value. Fix the colour in SVG.
5. Check that no icon looks like a real award, logo or person.
